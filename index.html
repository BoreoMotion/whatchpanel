What's WatchPanel?

WatchPanel is a Blender add-on that plays videos from your favorite video and streaming sites in a native Blender panel. Customize the playback controls, adjust the quality and speed, and watch with no ads.

Switch any Blender panel to WatchPanel, copy the link to a video or playlist, and click Play. The pop-up automatically pastes the link and detects the platform. Click once more to start watching. To play a local file or load a folder as a playlist, change the source manually in the same pop-up.

Supported platforms

  • Local Files

FAQ

  1. 01

    Does WatchPanel work on macOS or Linux?

    No. WatchPanel only works on Windows.

  2. 02

    Can I watch live streams?

    Yes. WatchPanel supports live streams from YouTube, Twitch, and Kick.

  3. 03

    Can I change the video quality or playback speed?

    Yes. You can choose from the quality levels the video source makes available, adjust playback speed, and customize the playback controls.

  4. 04

    Can I play my own video files?

    Yes. WatchPanel supports individual local videos and playlists, so you can watch files from your computer as well as videos from streaming sites.

  5. 05

    How do I install WatchPanel?

    Once you have the add-on file, open Blender’s Preferences, choose Install from Disk from the Add-ons menu, and select the file. Enable WatchPanel if it isn’t already enabled.
